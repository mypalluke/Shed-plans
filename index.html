FOUNDATION SPECIFICATIONS & FROST DEPTH

  • Excavation Depth: Footing bottom must be a minimum of 18 inches below grade, measured from the downhill exposed face into virgin, undisturbed soil.
  • Concrete Specification: Ready-mix or bag-mix achieving $f'_c \ge 3,000 \text{ psi}$ compressive strength at 28 days. Total required: 36 bags (80 lb).
  • Anchor Hardware: Cast-in-place Simpson Strong-Tie PBS66 elevated bases with 1" standoff clearance above concrete to prevent wood rot.

SLOPED TERRAIN LATERAL STABILITY NOTES

  • Post Height Threshold: Post height varies along slope from ~6" uphill to ~40" downhill, strictly remaining below the 48" non-engineered code limit.
  • Girder Splices: 6x6 girders must splice directly over the center of the 6x6 intermediate posts with full 2-3/4" minimum bearing for each timber end.
  • Cap Ties: Secure girders to posts with Simpson AC6 or PC66 heavy gauge caps.

LIVE / DEAD LOAD SPAN CAPACITIES

  • Clear Span: 5'-0" girder spacing creates two clear 4'-7" interior spans with 1'-0" end overhangs.
  • Prescriptive Capacity: NCRC Table R507.6 allows 2x6 No. 2 Southern Pine up to 9'-11" clear span for 40 psf live / 10 psf dead load. Over a 5' span, live load capacity easily exceeds 100+ psf, handling mowers, tools, and heavy storage.

FASTENER & SUBFLOOR SPECIFICATION

  • Moisture Expansion Gap: 1/8" gap required at all 4x8 panel butt joints to accommodate mountain humidity without buckling.

RAFTER SPAN & GEOMETRIC TAKE-OFF
